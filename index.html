Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

How does ordering work?

Send an enquiry with an outline of your idea. Nexora can then confirm the scope, price, estimated timeline and next steps before work begins.

Can I request custom features?

Yes. Packages are starting points. More specific requirements can be discussed and quoted separately.

Are domains and hosting included?

Not by default. Domain registration, hosting and paid third-party tools should be agreed separately before a project starts.

Can you make a private Discord server?

Yes. Discord setups can be tailored for private friend groups, public communities, gaming groups and other suitable uses.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size and complexity. You'll receive an estimated timeline once the requirements are understood.
